Quick info on hover

With nothing showing, hovering the notch drops it into the volume wide bar with your Wi-Fi network, Bluetooth device and battery, instead of the small bounce. Click it and each piece glides to its place in the panel as the notch opens, all inside the black shape, even mid-animation.

Hover the notch, then click it. Click outside it to close.

What it shows

  • Wi-Fi: the network's name, or the hotspot's with the hotspot icon. Not connected: a crossed-out Wi-Fi icon and "Not connected".
  • Bluetooth: the connected device (AirPods, a keyboard…). Nothing connected leaves it out, so the left wing is Wi-Fi alone.
  • Layout: like volume, the icon in the left wing (Wi-Fi) and the number in the right (battery). The network on the left of the body under them, the Bluetooth device on the right.
  • Battery: the same pill as the panel's header, with the bolt while charging.
  • Only when the notch has nothing else to show. Music, a notification, volume and the rest always win.

The motion

  • Hover: the notch grows down into the wide bar volume uses (wings plus a 26 pt body), or drops a chin when the menu bar has no room, on the same spring as other wings, with the text fading in at ≈0.15 s.
  • Click: the shape morphs into the panel while the Wi-Fi name, Bluetooth device and battery each glide on the same spring to their tile or header chip. The rest of the panel fades in behind them.
  • The island clips everything, so a piece that would be outside the shape mid-morph is hidden rather than floating over the desktop.

To decide

  1. Should it show at once, or after resting on the notch for a moment (say 0.3 s) so passing the pointer across the top doesn't flash it?
  2. On by default? Its switch would sit in Settings > Notch, and on the Islet page's feature list.
