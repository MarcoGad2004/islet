Islet Settings, redesigned

Pages in a sidebar, and one status line at the top that says whether everything is set up. Permissions are shown where they matter and stay out of the way while they're fine. Click around; the switch below shows the same window with problems.

What changes

  • The top of the sidebar is Islet's status. A green dot and "Everything is set up", or an orange dot and "2 things need attention". Its page lists only the problems, each with the fix.
  • No more lists of ticks. Permissions live on their own page as one line, "All 6 allowed", with the details folded away. A missing one is shown on the pages it breaks and gets a badge in the sidebar.
  • Face unlock gets a page of its own, with its three ticks folded into one status line.
  • Nothing is removed. Every setting from today's page is on one of these pages.

To decide

  1. Is Islet's status page the first page to open, or does it open on General?
  2. Notifications and Messages are on one page here. Do you want them split?
  3. Should the window be a bit wider: 720 × 520 here, against 460 × 720 today?

Building it

  • The native SwiftUI sidebar and grouped form, the same pieces System Settings uses. This keeps it light and dark and makes it look right on every macOS version Islet runs on.
  • The setup window keeps its own permissions step.
