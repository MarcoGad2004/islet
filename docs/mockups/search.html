Search from the notch

Press the search shortcut and the notch flows down into a search field, then bubbles out to the panel's width. Results for apps, Shortcuts, files and System Settings grow underneath as you type. Everything runs from the keyboard; the glass strip at the bottom carries what the keys do.

Type

Keys work here: ↑ ↓ to move, ⌘↑ ⌘↓ between sections, ↩ to open, ⌘↩ to show in Finder, Tab to complete, Esc to clear, Esc again to close. Press / to open it (the browser can't have ⌘Space; macOS keeps it for Spotlight).

Keys

KeyDoes
⌘SpaceOpens search from anywhere, full-screen apps too; again closes it. Changeable in Settings > General. Needs Spotlight's own shortcut moved first.
↑ ↓Moves the selection, wrapping past the ends. On an empty field, ↓ goes to the recent apps and ← → move along them.
⌘↑ ⌘↓Jumps to the first row of the previous or next section.
↩Opens the app, file or Settings page; runs the Shortcut; copies the answer.
⌘↩Shows the file or app in Finder.
⌘CCopies the selected file, or the answer.
TabFills the field with the selected name.
EscClears the text; on an empty field, closes.

Shapes and sizes

  • Body: 500 wide under the stem, like the expanded panel: top 16, bottom 30, 10 pt inner curves.
  • Height: field 52 + results (up to 8 rows, then it scrolls) + footer 28. Grows and shrinks in 0.22 s with no bounce while typing.
  • Opening: flow down at notch width (0.34 s), bubble out from 0.2 s, content in at 0.36 s. The cursor is in the field from the first frame.
  • Rows: top hit 52 (icon 32, name 15 semibold); others 34 (icon 22, name 13). Matched letters bright, the rest grey.
  • Footer: on the glass strip. Under it the black eases to 30% (not the usual 12%) so the hints stay readable.

Where results come from

  • Apps: the Spotlight index (application bundles), ranked by last opened.
  • Files: the Spotlight index through NSMetadataQuery, so the same files Spotlight finds.
  • Shortcuts: your Shortcuts library, run in the background.
  • Settings: a list of System Settings pages Islet keeps.
  • Calculator: Islet's own; no network.
  • Search in Spotlight: hands the text to Spotlight when ours falls short. To be proven in the spike.

Open questions

  • Shortcut: ⌘Space, which means moving Spotlight's, or something free like ⌥Space?
  • Web row: keep "Search the web", which only opens your browser, or leave it out?
  • From the panel: should typing while the panel is open start a search?
  • Left out for now: clipboard history, web results, contacts, mail and messages.
