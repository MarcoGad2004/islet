Charging animation

As the notch opens, the percentage rolls up from empty to the charge, and a battery fills alongside it in step. Once both land, the charging bolt fades in on top of the battery. Low battery does the same in reverse: it rolls down and drains, in red, with no bolt.

Timing

  • 0 s the notch springs out (same spring as now).
  • 0.1 s the battery fades in empty; the number drifts up into place, growing from 80% and sharpening out of a slight blur (0.45 s).
  • 0.15 – 1.3 s the number rolls, digits fading through the top and bottom edges, and the fill grows with it. It starts gently and slows right down for the last few percent.
  • 0.9 s a bigger, heavier bolt springs in over the battery, timed to settle at 1.3 s just as the fill stops: it overshoots about 8% with a slight twist and settles in about 0.4 s.
  • 3 s it hands back, as now.

What changes

  • The green bolt in the left wing becomes the battery page's battery glyph, with the bolt drawn on top of it at the end.
  • Fully charged rolls up to 100% and fills completely, then the bolt.
  • Low battery starts full and drains to 20% or 10%, rolling down, red, no bolt.
  • The chin keeps its label (“Charging · 1h 10m until full”) between the battery and the number.
