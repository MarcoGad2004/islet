Try: the timer and score run live. "Two at once" shows the newest in the wings and both cards in the panel. The card is built only from the activity's own text and buttons, read through Accessibility.

What Islet reads

  • Wings: the activity's two sides. The right one is text (the timer) and updates every second. The left is a picture Islet can't read, so the app's icon stands in.
  • Card: every text of the system's expanded card, in order, and its buttons. No colours or layout, so Islet lays it out: first text as the title, a short all-capitals word as an orange label, the value large on the right, the rest as lines.
  • Buttons: pressing "Complete" here presses the activity's own.

Open questions

  • The menu bar's copy: can System Settings hide it while Islet still reads it? To try on your Mac.
  • Reading the card: Islet has to open the system's card to read it. Moving it off screen worked once of two tries; it needs to work every time, or the card shows only the two sides.
  • Priority: above music in the wings (proposed). Music stays in the panel.
