The panel, flush with the top

When the menu bar is clear where the panel would go, the panel can grow straight out of the notch instead of hanging under it on a stem. Click "Open" to watch it, and switch the app in front to see it fall back to today's shape.

What changes

  • When the menu bar has room, the panel's top edge is the top of the screen. There's no stem and no inner curves; the top corners flare into the edge like the notch's own.
  • When it doesn't, the panel opens exactly as today, so a menu or status icon is never covered.
  • Room means 165 pt free on each side of the notch: half the panel's width beyond the notch, plus the usual 8 pt margin. The striped bands in the picture show it.
  • Opening works like the wings: the notch springs out sideways and down at once, with the same soft overshoot. Closing pulls it back into the notch.

A or B

  • A (recommended): the header moves up beside the camera, so the panel is 44 pt shorter. Left: the battery, update and Focus chips, or the back button on a detail page. Right: the album art, play and the gear. The song's name doesn't fit there; hovering the art shows it, and clicking it opens the player. The "Controls" label goes.
  • B: only the shape changes. The band beside the camera stays black and empty, and everything inside stays where it is today.
  • Pages with their own top (search's field, a conversation, the notification list) keep their layout under the band in both.

To decide

  1. A or B?
  2. If the menus change while the panel is open (another app comes to the front), should it morph to the other shape, or keep the shape it opened with until it closes?
  3. Without a notch nothing changes: the bubble and the menu bar card already float.
